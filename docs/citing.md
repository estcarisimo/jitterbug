# Citing Jitterbug

If you use Jitterbug in your research, please cite the PAM 2022 paper:

> Esteban Carisimo, Ricky K. P. Mok, David D. Clark and K. C. Claffy.
> *Jitterbug: A New Framework for Jitter-Based Congestion Inference.*
> Passive and Active Measurement (PAM 2022), Springer, pp. 155–179.
> [doi:10.1007/978-3-030-98785-5_7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-98785-5_7)
